# Levitating Knife — Universal Magnetic Puck

_A tiny wall-mounted magnetic knife holder, designed so the wood backing is invisible behind the knife. Each knife gets one or more small pucks that hide behind the bolster, so the blade looks like it's floating on the wall._

## TL;DR

- Universal **Ø25 × 12 mm** hardwood puck with a 1 mm leather face.
- Single magnet SKU: **N52 ring, 20 × 5 × 4.5 mm**. About **4–5 kg pull per puck**.
- **Three mounts from the same body**:
  - **A. VHB tape** — flat back, stick straight to the wall.
  - **B. Hidden dowel** — back socket friction-fits onto a dowel glued into a wall panel. Wood never visible.
  - **C. M4 screw** — most secure, survives bumps and earthquakes.
- Stack **1 puck per small knife**, up to **4 pucks for a 300 mm yanagiba**.
- Hand-tool buildable: 20 mm Forstner, 4.5 mm twist bit, plus one bit per mount.

## Print templates at 1:1

All SVGs carry `width`/`height` in mm. Print with "Actual Size" / "100%", **never** "Fit to page". Every template has a 50 mm scale bar at the bottom — verify with a ruler before drilling.
